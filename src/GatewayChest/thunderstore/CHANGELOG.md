# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First build. A hammer piece that opens a combined view of nearby chests, with
search, categories, sorting, a slot meter, withdraw into the player inventory,
and deposit into existing stacks or the first empty slot. Matching items share
one row. Shift-click a row to take a chosen amount. Drag from the backpack onto
the panel to store. The hub panel sits in the center of the screen, between the
backpack and the crafting column.
